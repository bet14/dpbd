§3 — LINDDUN Threat Modeling
7 threat categories applied to the Digital Financial Advisor system

🔗 L — Linkability

  • L1.1: Name + Location + Age → cross-dataset re-identification
  • L1.2: Gender + Income + Investment Pref → behavioral fingerprint
  • L1.3: Income + Assets + Debts → linkable to credit/tax databases
  • L1.4: Repeated "Seeking Further Consultation" → persistent anxiety pattern

🎯 I — Identifiability

  • I2.1: Full Name field → direct identifier
  • I2.2: Rare Location (Bouvet Island, Cocos Islands) → near-unique
  • I2.3: Professional titles in Name (DDS, MD) → cross-referenceable
  • I2.4: Income + Assets + Debts + Expenses → unique financial fingerprint

📝 N — Non-Repudiation

  • N3.1: Action logs permanently link user to financial decisions
  • N3.2: Feedback records create non-deniable behavioral history
  • N3.3: Audit trails may prevent exercising right to erasure (Art. 17)

👁️ D — Detectability

  • D4.1: "High ROI Investments" goal → wealth signal detectable
  • D4.2: API query patterns reveal financial interest categories
  • D4.3: Endpoint access frequency → detectable behavioral signature

📢 Di — Disclosure

  • Di5.1: Unencrypted financial data in transit/at rest
  • Di5.2: Insider access to full profile without need-to-know
  • Di5.3: Analytics exports containing individual-level records

😶 U — Unawareness

  • U6.1: Users unaware of behavioral profiling from advice interactions
  • U6.2: No visibility into how financial goals influence recommendations
  • U6.3: Lack of transparency on data retention periods

⚖️ NC — Non-Compliance

  • NC7.1: No explicit legal basis documented per processing activity
  • NC7.2: Missing RoPA — GDPR Art. 30
  • NC7.3: No consent withdrawal mechanism for behavioral data
  • NC7.4: Cross-border transfers without SCCs/adequacy decisions
  • NC7.5: No DPIA despite high-risk financial profiling

§5 — ARX Decision Rationale
Input specifications, transformation choices, and privacy model justifications

⚙️ Input Specification Decisions

Attribute → DecisionRationale
Name → Identifying / SuppressedDirect identifier — no analytical value. Removed per GDPR Art. 25. Suppressed to * in output.
Location → Identifying / SuppressedCity+Country near-unique for rare territories (Bouvet Island, Cocos Islands). Suppressed to prevent geographic re-identification.
Age → Quasi-ID / GeneralizedExact age enables re-identification with Gender+Location. Generalized to [0,80[ covering all ages 18–70.
Gender → Quasi-ID / Generalized3-level hierarchy: exact → Person → *. Retained at level 0 for most records; suppressed for rare combinations.
Income → Quasi-ID / GeneralizedPrecise income (e.g., 8576.35) matchable to payroll records. Generalized into 3 financial brackets: [0,5000[, [5000,8000[, ≥8000.
Assets → Quasi-ID / GeneralizedHigh-value assets create rare profiles. Generalized to [0,80000[ and ≥80000 preserving wealth-tier analytics.
Debts → Quasi-ID / SuppressedMany records have Debts=0 (distinctive). Combined with other attrs, creates highly unique combinations. Fully suppressed.
Monthly Expenses → Quasi-ID / SuppressedHigh-granularity continuous variable. Linkable to bank/e-commerce history. Suppressed to prevent behavioral fingerprinting.

🔒 Privacy Models Applied

🔢 k-Anonymity (k=5)
  • Every quasi-ID combination appears ≥5 times
  • Prevents singling out individuals
  • Applied to: Age + Gender + Income + Assets
  • 30 records suppressed where k<5 unavoidable
📊 l-Diversity (implicit)
  • Sensitive attrs (Financial Goal, Advice Type) maintain diversity within equivalence classes
  • Prevents attribute disclosure attacks
  • Verified through ARX solution explorer
🎯 Data Quality Model
  • Sensitive attrs (Investment Pref, Advice Type, Goal) retained without generalization
  • Insensitive behavioral attrs fully retained
  • Balance: privacy protection vs. analytical utility
⚡ Suppression Strategy
  • 6% suppression rate (30/500) — acceptable threshold
  • Targets outliers: rare locations, extreme financial values
  • Preserves 94% of dataset for downstream analytics

🔐 Key Technical Solutions — Deep Dive

🔑 Pseudonymization at Ingestion
  • Replace Name with UUID at data entry point
  • Name↔UUID mapping in access-controlled identity vault
  • HMAC-SHA256 with secret salt for internal references
  • Ref: GDPR Art. 25; OWASP A02:2021
📊 Differential Privacy for Income
  • Add Laplace noise (ε=0.5) before analytics storage
  • Round to nearest $500 bracket + noise injection
  • Expose Income only through DP-protected aggregate queries
  • Ref: NIST SP 800-188; Apple DP whitepaper
🌍 Location Generalization
  • Store only country/region (never city-level)
  • Rare territories (pop. <50k) → "Other/Small Territory"
  • Geo-masking: random offset within administrative region
  • Consent-gated precision for tax jurisdiction use cases
🔒 Tokenization for Financial Fields
  • Replace Income/Assets/Debts with vault tokens (HashiCorp/AWS)
  • Federated architecture: never join all financial fields
  • SMPC for cross-institutional analytics
  • Ref: PCI-DSS v4.0 Req.3; OWASP A04:2021
⏰ Temporal Data Expiry (TTL)
  • TTL 90 days on behavioral logs (Action, Feedback)
  • Only aggregated summaries retained long-term
  • Blind signatures for unlinkable audit logs
  • Ref: GDPR Art. 17 (Right to Erasure)
🤖 Synthetic Data Generation
  • Replace Monthly Expenses with GAN/VAE synthetic equivalents
  • Preserve statistical distributions without individual records
  • Tools: Gretel.ai, SDV library
  • Ref: ENISA PETs Guidelines 2022

📄 7.3 — Privacy Policy (1-page)

Digital Financial Advisor — Privacy Policy

Data Controller: Digital Financial Advisor Platform | Contact: privacy@dfa-platform.com

What we collect: Name, Age, Location, Gender, Income, Assets, Debts, Monthly Expenses, Investment Preferences, Advice interactions, and Feedback.

Why we collect it: To provide personalized financial advisory services (contract necessity), improve our platform (legitimate interest), and comply with legal obligations.

How long we keep it: Profile data: service duration + 30 days. Behavioral logs: 90 days TTL, then aggregated only. Anonymized research data: indefinitely.

Who we share with: No third-party sharing without explicit consent. Anonymized aggregates may be used for research. Cross-border transfers protected by SCCs.

Your rights (GDPR Art. 15–22): Access · Rectification · Erasure · Restriction · Portability · Objection · Withdraw consent at any time.

Security: AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, pseudonymization, ABAC access controls.
